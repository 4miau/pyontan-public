## Windows app

pyontan lives in your tray. Start it with Windows and it stays out of the way until you need it. Every feature has a hotkey, and none of it needs a browser tab.

### OCR Capture
Reads Japanese straight off your screen: games, visual novels, manga, video subtitles, images, anything you can't copy as text. Press Alt+L and drag a box. Dictionary mode looks up the word you click, Translator mode translates the whole line. Live Mode keeps reading one spot as it changes, like a game's text box.

### Live Audio Translation
Listens to what your PC is playing (streams, videos, anime, games, calls) and shows what's said as subtitles, in Japanese with the translation. It works on anything coming out of your speakers or headphones, and doesn't use the microphone. The speech recognition runs on your own computer, with an add-on for NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics cards to make it faster.

### Batch Translate
Add a folder of manga, manhwa or comic pages, or any screenshots. Every bubble is read and translated page by page, and on Premium the translation is lettered back into the bubbles. Pages in Japanese, Korean, Chinese or English all work.

### Game text
Reads a visual novel or game with each line translated as it comes up, every word clickable. The window sits on top of the game and never takes its focus, so your next click still moves the story on. pyontan doesn't hook the game itself: your text hooker finds the lines and pyontan reads them from it.

### Song Lyrics
Play a song in Spotify or your browser and press Alt+M. pyontan finds the published lyrics and follows the song line by line, with furigana and a translation. Click any word to look it up.

### Quick Translate
Copy Japanese anywhere and a popup answers, or type a line and see it translated as you go. Use the hosted translation, or your own key.

### Reading Assist
Paste a whole passage and read it with furigana over the kanji you don't know yet, and none over the ones you do. Open any word for its full card.

### Dictionary
Kanji, kana, romaji or English all work, so no Japanese keyboard is needed. Readings, meanings, pitch accent, every form of a verb, kanji with their parts, example sentences, and recordings of real people saying the word. Meanings can show in your own language where the dictionary has them.

### Words, History and Anki
Save a word with the sentence you found it in, and send it to Anki in one click. Every word you looked up and every line Live Audio heard is kept and searchable. pyontan can learn which words you already know from Anki, WaniKani or Renshuu, so it only helps with the rest.

### Made for long sessions
- Potato Mode for older PCs: big models unload when idle, and OCR and audio only load once you use them
- Its overlays can hide from screenshots and screen recordings
- Themes, backgrounds and seven interface languages

## Study

In the app and on the website, with the same progress on both.

- **Grammar lessons** in JLPT order from N5 to N1, each building on the ones before, with an explanation, examples, tables that show how a verb or adjective changes, and a quiz
- **Reviews** that bring each grammar point back just before you'd forget it. They're typed, and when you're stuck the hints come one step at a time instead of the answer. Each point grows through moon stages until it's mastered
- **Vocabulary, kana, kanji and counters**, and the words you look up go into your reviews too
- **Listening, sentences and JLPT-style tests**, with mock exams on the paid plans
- **Games**: kana sprint, word match and crosswords
- **AI Conversation**: chat in Japanese with someone set to your level, a friend or someone you'd meet in Japan, casual or keigo. It keeps the chat going and points out a real mistake in a short note
- **A placement test** to start at the right lesson

The first N5 lessons are free.

## Reading and notes

- **Manga library**: finished chapters go to your library on the website. Read them in any browser, phone included, with each bubble's line and translation a tap away. Switch to the original, and carry on from the page you stopped at
- **Notebook**: your own notes, plus the words, sentences, lessons and AI Tutor answers you saved. The same Notebook in the app and on the website
- **AI Tutor**: ask anything about Japanese ("は or が here?") and get a plain answer with examples, the words in it and the lessons it's about

## Website

For a Mac, a Chromebook, a phone or a PC you can't install on. [pyontan.com](https://pyontan.com) has the Dictionary, Translator, Conjugations, Study, Notebook and your manga library. Batch Translate works there too, with no app: upload pages and they're read, translated and lettered. It installs on your phone like an app.

## Android

Everything on the website as an app, plus:

- pyontan in every app's text menu and Share sheet
- A Translate tile in quick settings
- **Screen translate**: translate Japanese in any app or game on your phone. Drag a box round it, or translate the whole screen at once. The text is read on the phone and the screenshot never leaves it

On [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.pyontan) (early access), or as an APK from [pyontan.com/download](https://pyontan.com/download).

## Browser extension

**pyontan Hop**, for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox. It works with your account, or with the Windows app when it's running.

- Translates Twitch and YouTube chat as it scrolls by (livestreams and VODs), and Kick, TwitCasting and Niconico Live once you turn them on
- Hold a key and point at Japanese on any page for a word card
- Translates a whole page, or the text you highlight
- Translates comics on the sites you turn it on for, right over the bubbles, and can save the chapter to your library

[Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/pyontan-web-extension/ndmcijolccjcgmfpdijichkkggckiijn) · [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/pyontan-hop/)

## Account and sync

One account, the same progress on your PC, the website and your phone: Study, saved words, the Notebook and your library. Sign in with email, Discord or Patreon, with passkeys and two-step sign-in in Settings > Security.

Your lookups, saved words and Live Audio history stay on your PC unless you turn on sync.
