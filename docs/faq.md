# FAQ

### Is pyontan free?
Yes. Every tool is in the free app, with no card needed. Paid plans raise the daily limits, open up more of Study and add storage and sync. See [pyontan.com/pricing](https://pyontan.com/pricing).

### Is it open source?
No. pyontan is closed source. This repo is its public page: the details, the docs and a place to report bugs.

### Is it safe?
Download it only from [pyontan.com/download](https://pyontan.com/download) or the stores linked there. Each download lists a SHA-256 so you can check the file is the one I published. The Windows app isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may warn you the first time you open it: press More info, then Run anyway. More in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

### Is it on Mac or Linux?
The app is Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) for now. On a Mac, a Chromebook or Linux, [the website](https://pyontan.com) has the Dictionary, Translator, Study and your manga library, and the browser extension works there too.

### Is there a phone app?
Yes, on Android: [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.pyontan) (early access), or the APK from [pyontan.com/download](https://pyontan.com/download). On an iPhone, the website installs to your home screen.

### Do I need an account?
For Study, sync and the hosted translation, yes. Signing up is free.

### Can I use my own translation key?
Yes, on every plan, Free included: DeepL, Google, or an AI model from OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek or OpenRouter. Local Ollama and LM Studio work too.

### What are Mochi?
Credits you buy once, on any plan. They pick up where the day's amount stops and never expire. [pyontan.com/mochi](https://pyontan.com/mochi)

### Where does my data live?
On your device, unless you turn on sync to your account. Text you translate goes to the translation service you picked. [Privacy policy](https://pyontan.com/privacy)

### Does Live Audio use my microphone?
No. It listens to what your PC is playing, and the speech recognition runs on your own computer.

### Can it read manhwa or Chinese comics?
Yes. Batch Translate reads pages in Japanese, Korean, Chinese or English and translates them into your language.

### Which languages is it in?
The app and website are in English, Spanish, French, Dutch, Japanese, Russian and Arabic. Study lessons are in English.

### Who makes it?
Just me. Bug reports, questions and ideas go to [feedback](https://pyontan.com/feedback), an [issue](../../../issues) here, or [Discord](https://discord.gg/Pz3gCFPejV).
